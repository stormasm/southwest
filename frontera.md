

The largest cities directly on the U.S.–Mexico border form major binational pairs. On the U.S. side, the largest border cities are San Diego (California) and El Paso (Texas). On the Mexican side, their respective twin cities are Tijuana (Baja California) and Ciudad Juárez (Chihuahua), which are significantly larger in population

### Major U.S. Border Cities

- San Diego, California: Population over 1.3 million (part of the massive San Diego–Tijuana metro area).
- El Paso, Texas: Population around 680,000, sitting directly on the border across from Ciudad Juárez.
- Laredo, Texas: Population around 260,000, a key inland port of entry on the Rio Grande.
- Brownsville, Texas: Population near 187,000, located at the southernmost tip of Texas.
- McAllen, Texas: Population over 145,000, situated in the lower Rio Grande Valley.

### Major Mexican Border Cities

- Tijuana, Baja California: Population over 1.8 million, the busiest land-border crossing area in the world paired with San Diego.
- Ciudad Juárez, Chihuahua: Population over 1.5 million, sitting directly across from El Paso.
- Mexicali, Baja California: Population over 850,000, located across from Calexico, California.
- Reynosa, Tamaulipas: Population over 700,000, located across from McAllen, Texas.
- Matamoros, Tamaulipas: Population over 520,000, located across from Brownsville, Texas.

### Safest Mexican - US Border City

Piedras Negras, located in the northern Mexican state of Coahuila directly across the border from Eagle Pass, Texas, is widely considered the safest Mexican border city. According to Mexico's National Public Safety Survey (INEGI), it consistently ranks as the safest municipality along the U.S.–Mexico border and one of the safest cities in the country.

### Why Piedras Negras Ranks Safe

- Low Crime Metrics: Government data and local reports show significantly lower rates of violent crime and cartel conflict here compared to other border hubs like Tijuana, Ciudad Juárez, or Matamoros.
- State Support: The state of Coahuila maintains robust security coordination, keeping transit corridors and ports of entry stable.
- Cross-Border Ties: It shares a quiet, integrated economy with Eagle Pass, Texas, featuring smooth international bridge crossings

### Other Relatively Safe Border Locations

- Tecate: Located in Baja California just 45 minutes from San Diego, this small pueblo m mágico is frequently recommended as a calmer, safer day-trip border town.
- Boquillas del Carmen: A tiny, rural village across from Big Bend National Park in Texas. While it is a remote river crossing rather than a major city, it is widely viewed as a very low-risk, friendly destination for daytime visits
