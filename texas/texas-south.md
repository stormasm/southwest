The largest city in South Texas is **San Antonio**.

### **Key Details**

* **Population:** \~1.55 to 1.57 million residents (making it the 2nd-largest city in Texas overall and the 7th-largest in the United States).  
* **Metro Population:** The San Antonio–New Braunfels metropolitan area has over 2.7 million residents.

### **Other Major Cities in South Texas**

If considering South Texas by sub-regions (such as the Rio Grande Valley or the Coastal Bend), other prominent cities include:

> 1. **Corpus Christi** (\~317,000 residents) – Largest coastal city in South Texas.  
> 2. **Laredo** (\~270,000 residents) – Largest inland port city along the U.S.–Mexico border.  
> 3. **Brownsville** (\~193,000 residents) – Largest city in the lower Rio Grande Valley.  
> 4. **McAllen** (\~152,000 residents) – Major commercial center in the Rio Grande Valley (metro population near 900,000).
