*User prompt: what is the main intl airport in the rio grande valley of texas*

Response: The Rio Grande Valley (RGV) of South Texas is served by **three main commercial airports**, each filling a key role depending on destination and carrier preferences:

### **1\. Valley International Airport (HRL) — *Harlingen, TX***

> * **Status:** Largest airport by land area in the Rio Grande Valley.  
> * **Best Known For:** Serving as the main "Gateway to South Padre Island" and handling the region's largest volume of air cargo.  
> * **Key Carriers:** Southwest Airlines, American Airlines, United Express, Delta (seasonal), and Sun Country (seasonal).

### **2\. McAllen International Airport (MFE) — *McAllen, TX***

> * **Status:** Busiest airport by passenger volume in the region.  
> * **Best Known For:** Central location within the main urban population center of the RGV, offering convenience for business travel and shopping.  
> * **Key Carriers:** American Airlines, United Express, Allegiant Air, Aeroméxico (direct flights to Mexico City), and Delta.

### **3\. Brownsville/South Padre Island International Airport (BRO) — *Brownsville, TX***

> * **Status:** Closest commercial airport to the U.S.–Mexico border at Matamoros, as well as the SpaceX Launch Facility (Starbase) at Boca Chica.  
> * **Key Carriers:** American Airlines, United Express, and Avelo Airlines.

### **Summary**

> * If you are looking for the **largest regional gateway facility**, it is **Valley International Airport (HRL)** in Harlingen.  
> * If you are looking for the **busiest commercial hub**, it is **McAllen International Airport (MFE)**.
