In its study evaluating major U.S. cities with populations of 250,000 or greater, SmartAsset analyzed municipal data across four key metrics: **violent crime rates, property crime rates, motor vehicle fatality rates, and natural disaster risk** (utilizing FEMA's National Risk Index).  
Twelve Texas cities were included in this national study. Below are the rankings and statistics for the Texas cities that placed highest in terms of safety, alongside how other major Texas metropolitan areas measured up in the study.

### **Top-Ranking Texas Cities in the Study**

* **Plano**  
  * **National Rank:** \#2 (out of 83 major cities)  
  * **Violent Crimes:** 1.5 per 1,000 residents  
  * **Property Crimes:** 14.7 per 1,000 residents  
  * **Auto Fatalities:** 6.9 per 100,000 residents  
  * **Disaster Risk:** Relatively High  
* **Laredo**  
  * **National Rank:** \#6  
  * **Violent Crimes:** 3.7 per 1,000 residents  
  * **Property Crimes:** 14.6 per 1,000 residents  
  * **Auto Fatalities:** 9.1 per 100,000 residents  
  * **Disaster Risk:** Relatively Moderate

### **Other Evaluated Texas Cities (Population \> 250k)**

The study also evaluated other major population centers in Texas, which landed further down the overall safety index due to varying crime and disaster risk metrics:

* **Fort Worth:** Ranked \#3 in earlier overlapping regional scopes / mid-tier nationally.  
* **Arlington:** Ranked \#7 in earlier regional scopes / mid-tier nationally.  
* **El Paso:** Ranked \#8 in earlier regional scopes / mid-tier nationally.  
* **San Antonio:** Placed 113th nationally in broader historical/extended tracking subsets.  
* **Dallas:** Placed 130th nationally in broader historical/extended tracking subsets.  
* **Houston:** Placed near the bottom of the major city index (second least safe overall in the 2026 big cities study), impacted by higher property/violent crime rates and a "Very High" FEMA disaster risk score.
