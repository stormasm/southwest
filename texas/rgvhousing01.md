Response: When looking for reasonably priced extended stay options in the Rio Grande Valley (RGV) of Texas, most of the regional inventory concentrates around **McAllen** (the economic hub of the Valley), offering a mix of budget-focused kitchenettes and more traditional apartment-style long-term setups.

Several well-rated and budget-conscious extended stay options across the region include:

### **Budget & Value Extended Stays**

> * **Studio 6 (McAllen)**  
  * **Location:** 700 Savannah Ave, McAllen  
  * **Features:** Designed explicitly for budget-conscious long-term travelers. It features modern studio rooms with fully equipped kitchenettes (microwaves, refrigerators, stovetops), free Wi-Fi, an outdoor pool, and on-site guest laundry facilities.  
  * **Vibe:** Straightforward, highly functional, and one of the lowest weekly/monthly rate structures in the immediate area.  
> * **Victoria Palms Inn & Suites (Alamo / McAllen Area)**  
  * **Location:** 3309 E Expressway 83, Alamo (just east of McAllen)  
  * **Features:** Operates heavily as an extended stay and winter visitor community. Offers fully-furnished apartment layouts with full kitchens, dishware, weekly housekeeping, high-speed Wi-Fi, gated security, heated pools, and hot tubs without the hassle of setting up local utility accounts.  
  * **Vibe:** Resort-style community feel, popular for longer multi-week or multi-month stays.

> * **Homewood Suites by Hilton (McAllen)**  
  * **Location:** 3801 Expressway 83, McAllen

  * **Vibe:** Premium extended stay comfort with robust weekly rates.

### **Tips for Booking in the RGV:**

> 1. **Ask for Weekly/Monthly Rates:** If you are staying for more than 7 consecutive days, always call the property directly rather than booking standard nightly rates online. Many properties in the RGV offer heavily discounted tier pricing for multi-week stays.  
> 2. **Location Proximity:** If you want to navigate without leaning heavily on a car, properties located centrally near **Expresway 83 (Jimmie Feldman Freeway)** or close to 10th Street in McAllen put you within walking distance or short transit hops of major grocery stores (like H-E-B), dining, and medical districts.
