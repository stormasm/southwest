The population of the Rio Grande Valley (RGV) in South Texas varies slightly depending on whether you count just the Texas counties or the broader transborder metropolitan region:

### **1\. U.S. Side (Texas RGV)**

* **Population:** **\~1.45 million people**  
* **Counties Included:** Hidalgo, Cameron, Starr, and Willacy.  
* **Key Breakdowns:**  
  * **Hidalgo County** (McAllen, Edinburg, Mission) accounts for the vast majority with over 870,000 residents.  
  * **Cameron County** (Brownsville, Harlingen) holds over 420,000 residents.  
  * **Starr & Willacy Counties** make up the remainder.

### **2\. Binational Metropolitan Region (RGV \+ Border Cities in Mexico)**

* **Population:** **Over 2.7 to 3 million people**  
* If you include the contiguous Mexican border cities directly connected to the Valley's economy and urban footprint (such as **Reynosa** and **Matamoros**), the cross-border urban area forms one of the largest metropolitan regions along the U.S.–Mexico border.
