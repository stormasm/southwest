
## El Paso

Plano, El Paso, and Laredo rank as the safest major large cities in Texas with populations over 250,000, according to national safety analyses evaluating violent crime, property crime, and traffic safety. For broader metropolitan safety, fast-growing mid-sized suburbs like Frisco, McKinney, and Sugar Land consistently report the lowest per-capita crime rates in the state.

El Paso: Consistently scores as one of the safest large cities in the United States, with a violent crime rate far below the average for cities of its size (> 500,000 residents)
